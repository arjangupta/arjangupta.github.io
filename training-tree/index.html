Evidence, not experience points.

Inferred foundations are shown as context, never counted as verified completions. Active skills remain trainable while prerequisites await confirmation. Supporting drills are useful routes, not mandatory physiological gates.

    How progression works · methodology & data

    Each criterion is an application coaching checkpoint, not a scientifically validated universal prerequisite. Parallel routes remain visible. Only explicit criteria met or a verified baseline complete a node; three successful assessment days confer mastery. Node percentage measures demonstrated milestones, not athletic proficiency. Duration milestones don’t imply equal difficulty.

    October 2026 baseline is athlete-reported, with no exact day supplied. A six-rep dip set does not establish stable support, and three assisted transitions do not establish symmetrical technique. Neither is marked complete.

    Keep attempts fresh and controlled. Use an appropriate clear space and secure equipment. Stop for pain or loss of safe control. This map is a skill reference, not an instruction to add training volume.

06 / DATA NOTES

Useful, because it’s traceable.

Snapshot through October 19, 2026. Records available here begin in August 2025; earlier 2025 training is not represented.

What counts as a result?

Direct log: the notes state the load or result. Plan + confirmation: the workout prescribes the reps and the notes confirm completion; that inference is labeled. Block summary: a retrospective summary, with less precise dates. Test: the year-end baseline’s testing result. Blank notes and future prescriptions never count as completed performance.

Rep counts remain blank when completion is not clear. The October 185 lb deadlift is an exposure with unknown reps, not the prescribed five-rep set. March’s primary pull-up notes say +22.5×4; the monitoring summary says +25×4. This dashboard uses the primary notes.

How are the graphs calculated?

Load is external barbell load; pull-ups and dips show added load only, not bodyweight plus load. No bodyweight ratios are calculated without matched bodyweight measurements. Points are individual selected exposures; no interpolated curve or attendance total is implied.

Estimated 1RM uses Epley: load × (1 + reps/30), with singles retained at their measured load. It is available only for barbell observations with known reps. It does not adjust for RPE, tempo, fatigue or technique. Use it as a rough comparison, not a tested max or proof of a PR.

Cardio bars use explicitly reported minutes. The August 26 workout prescribed 45 minutes, but its notes report 29 in Zone 2 and 11 in Zone 3: the chart shows those 40 classified minutes. Unknown zone splits remain gray. Activities and heart-rate zone definitions differ.

Dates, coverage and limitations

Dates usually follow workout labels or the programmed weekday within its folder. Workouts sometimes moved to another day; these labels do not certify execution dates. Year-end testing is plotted at December month-end because the baseline does not establish exact execution days. Block summaries are plotted at block-end.

The review fetched 344 documents across the 2025 Mesocycles and both 2026 macrocycle folders, including plans, summaries, logs and empty templates. The page curates 71 strength observations and 7 timed cardio sessions, rather than treating every prescription as a completed workout. Session totals, training tonnage and completion percentages would require a fuller structured log.

Skill indicators are custom qualitative milestones. The supplied Overcoming Gravity charts informed progression context; no full chart or unsupported numerical skill rank is reproduced. Hold standards vary, so a long swinging tuck is not compared directly with a short motionless advanced tuck.
